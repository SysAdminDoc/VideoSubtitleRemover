# Alpine interview source prompt

## Intended use

Photorealistic demonstration media for professional software screenshots.

## Prompt

Scene: a wide 16:9 documentary interview frame at an alpine overlook shortly after sunrise, with a fictional adult outdoor filmmaker standing in three-quarter view beside a calm blue lake and layered mountain ridges. The person is naturally posed and looking slightly off camera, wearing an understated charcoal field jacket with realistic fabric texture. Soft natural rim light, believable skin detail, subtle atmospheric haze, restrained cinematic color grade, captured like a real full-frame camera photograph with a 50 mm lens.

Composition: polished editorial framing with the subject in the upper-left third and open landscape across the frame. Keep the lower 28 percent visually quiet and moderately dark so crisp subtitle text can be added later without covering the subject. Preserve natural depth and realistic proportions.

Constraints: no text, no subtitles, no logos, no brand marks, no watermark, no interface, no frames, no borders, no fantasy elements, no plastic skin, no exaggerated HDR, and no oversaturated colors.

Output intent: premium, credible sample footage that would look at home in a serious commercial video-editing product README and app preview.
